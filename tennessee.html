Home care agencies in Tennessee — by the numbers.

Tennessee's file covers 120 home care agencies across 20 cities — the Nashville metro (36), Knoxville (25), Memphis (23), Chattanooga (18), the Tri-Cities (11), and Clarksville (7). It is a relationship market with thin digital plumbing: of the 43 agencies whose booking status has been verified, 40 take no bookings online, and only 13 agencies in the file publish an email address.

Methodology

Drawn from the OwnerFlight Tennessee database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Tennessee licenses these agencies as Personal Support Services Agencies, but the Dept. of Health licensure portal was not directly accessible during the build, so license numbers (19) come from agency pages and NPI records, and no source published license issue dates — the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here yet; a portal pass is the file's first refresh priority. Instagram verification was systematic only in Chattanooga and the Tri-Cities, so Tennessee publishes no statewide Instagram figure. Online booking is verified on 43 records. Tennessee is a live OwnerFlight market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
