Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Minnesota database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Minnesota licenses home care providers through the Department of Health (comprehensive and basic classes, recorded in the file where known), but MDH publishes no downloadable roster and no reachable license issue dates, so license numbers appear on 3 records and the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here yet; a dedicated MDH directory pull is the logged unlock. Online booking is verified on 67 records. Minnesota is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
