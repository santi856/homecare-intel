Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel North Carolina database (October 2026): home care agencies identified from public records and directories and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. North Carolina licenses home care agencies through DHSR; license numbers are recorded on 48 records, but DHSR does not expose license issue dates in a machine-readable list, so the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire yet. Online booking has been verified on 16 records so far, and Instagram absence was not certified beyond the 3 handles found — no statewide Instagram statistic is published for North Carolina. North Carolina is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
