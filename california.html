Home care agencies in California — by the numbers.

California's file covers 113 home care agencies across 39 cities, from Los Angeles (17) and San Diego (14) down to one-agency towns. It is our most conservative file: booking status is verified on a first subset of agencies and Instagram presence is not yet scored, so California's priority scores run lower than other states' until verification catches up — the agencies underneath are real, deduplicated, and cross-checked.

Methodology

Drawn from the OwnerFlight California database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. California scoring is deliberately conservative in this pass: Instagram presence was not scored (no Instagram statistic is published for California, because it was not verified), and online booking was verified on a subset of agency sites. License (HCO) numbers are recorded on 24 records, taken from the agencies' own published pages. California is a live OwnerFlight market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
