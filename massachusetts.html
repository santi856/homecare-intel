Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Massachusetts database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Massachusetts does not state-license non-medical home care, so the license field is empty statewide by design and the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here. Instagram presence was scored 0 statewide in this pass — only one location-level handle could be verified and no last-post date could be confirmed anywhere — so no Instagram statistic is published for Massachusetts; a verification pass is the file's biggest logged score unlock. Online booking is verified on 76 records. Massachusetts is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
