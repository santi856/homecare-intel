Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Washington database (October 2026): home care agencies identified from public records and directories and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Washington's Department of Health publishes license numbers inconsistently in public sources — only 7 are recorded here — and does not publish license issue dates in the sources used, so the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire in Washington; Washington scores rest on booking, social, and review signals only. Booking status is verified on 81 records and counting; records whose booking status could not be verified were left blank and excluded from the statistic rather than estimated. Washington is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
