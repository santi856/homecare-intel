Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel New Jersey database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. New Jersey registers Health Care Service Firms but does not publish registration issue dates in the sources used, so the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here; scores rest on booking, social, and review signals. Google ratings are verified on 59 of the 120 records — the rest were left blank rather than filled from non-Google sources — and online booking is verified on 93. New Jersey is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
