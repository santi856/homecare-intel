Home care agencies in Michigan — by the numbers.

Michigan's file covers 118 home care agencies across 49 cities — the Detroit metro (38), Kalamazoo with Flint / Saginaw (25), Grand Rapids (23), Ann Arbor (17), and Lansing (15). It is a slightly more digital market than its neighbors: of the 67 agencies whose booking status has been verified, 8 offer online booking — modest, but the highest count in this wave.

Methodology

Drawn from the OwnerFlight Michigan database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Michigan does not state-license non-medical home care, so the license field is empty statewide by design — no NPI was ever recorded as a license. Google ratings are verified on 55 of the 118 records; figures from non-Google aggregators were never substituted. Online booking is verified on 67 records and counting. Michigan is a live OwnerFlight market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
