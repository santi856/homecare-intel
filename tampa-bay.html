Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Florida database (October 2026): licensed home care agencies identified from Florida license records and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated. Agencies that serve more than one city are counted once in the regional total and under each city they serve in the breakdown above. Single-city pages count agencies by their primary listed city. Florida is HomeCare Intel's live market; additional states launch as each is validated.
