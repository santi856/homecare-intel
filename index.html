The problem

Your team’s best hours belong in outreach—not in twelve open tabs trying to verify who runs an agency.

01 / SEARCH

Maps is not a database

Static lists go stale

Administrators change. Agencies close. New operators launch. Within months, yesterday’s research becomes today’s bounce list.

Done-For-You is $800 per month, or $8,000 annually paid upfront (saving $1,600 versus monthly billing; annual subscriptions renew each year): we email the agency owners in your market, work every reply, and book calls on your calendar — if the first launched service month produces zero positive replies, monthly DFY clients get the next service month free and prepaid annual clients carry the same zero-reply protection (annual prepay already includes two months free). There is no per-record charge.

What information is included for each agency?

Agency name, city, street address, phone, website, state license number, owner or administrator name, Google rating and review count, Instagram handle and recent activity, online-booking signal, a 0–100 priority score, a tier (HOT, WARM, NURTURE, or COLD), and a plain-English reason for the score. Fields that cannot be verified are left blank rather than estimated.

Who is OwnerFlight for?

Any business that sells into home care: home care and senior care marketing specialists, software and EHR vendors, staffing platforms, billing and RCM firms, answering services, consultants, and franchise development firms. It is not a consumer directory and it is not for agencies looking to be listed.

What does Done-For-You (DFY) include?

We email the licensed home care agency owners in your market using the verified contacts in our database, work every reply, and book calls on your calendar. You approve the outreach angle before launch, replies are handled and reported on the agreed campaign schedule, and you receive a weekly sent / replies / booked report. Full database access for your market is included. Guarantee: monthly DFY receives a free second month if the first fully launched month generates zero positive replies; prepaid annual DFY carries the same zero-reply protection (annual prepay already includes two months free).

How is this different from Apollo or ZoomInfo?

Broad B2B platforms are built to cover every industry. OwnerFlight is built around one vertical and includes license context plus senior-care-specific prospecting signals — such as visible Instagram activity and online-booking gaps — that broad databases typically do not carry. What format do I receive?

A clean CSV that can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets and imported into most CRM and outreach platforms.

How often do I receive updates?

After we verify a completed checkout and confirm the selected markets, data files are emailed manually — any 3 states you choose on DIY, every live state on All-Access. After that you receive the Hot 10 brief for your states every Monday and fully refreshed, re-verified files on the 1st of each month. New states are added as they are verified: All-Access includes them at no extra cost, and on DIY you can add a state anytime for $75/mo.

How does priority scoring work?

Every agency gets a 0–100 priority score from our own scoring model. It weighs the signals that say an agency is ready to buy — how established it is, whether it can take an online booking, how visible it is online — and every row shows the reason for its score in plain English. Scores land in tiers: HOT, WARM, NURTURE, COLD. The recipe itself is ours; what it produces is what you see in the public preview.

What counts as a trigger?

Triggers include a newly issued license, a meaningful Google rating change, social activity going quiet, or a visible conversion gap such as no online booking. Each Hot 10 entry includes the change, when it was detected, and a suggested outreach angle.
