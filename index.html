What information is included for each agency?

Agency name, city, street address, phone, website, state license number, owner or administrator name, Google rating and review count, Instagram handle and recent activity, online-booking signal, a 0–100 priority score, a tier (HOT, WARM, NURTURE, or COLD), and a plain-English reason for the score. Fields that cannot be verified are left blank rather than estimated.

Who is HomeCare Intel for?

Any business that sells into home care: home care and senior care marketing specialists, software and EHR vendors, staffing platforms, billing and RCM firms, answering services, consultants, and franchise development firms. It is not a consumer directory and it is not for agencies looking to be listed.

What does Done-For-You (DFY) include?

We email the licensed home care agency owners in your market using the verified contacts in our database, work every reply, and book calls on your calendar. You approve the outreach angle before launch, every reply is forwarded to you the same day, and you get a weekly sent / replies / booked report. Full database access is included. Guarantee: zero positive replies in month one, month two is free.

How is this different from Apollo or ZoomInfo?

Broad B2B platforms are built to cover every industry. HomeCare Intel is built around one vertical and includes license context plus senior-care-specific prospecting signals — such as visible Instagram activity and online-booking gaps — that broad databases typically do not carry.

What format do I receive?

A clean CSV that can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets and imported into most CRM and outreach platforms.

How often do I receive updates?

The full database for every live state is delivered the day you subscribe. After that you receive the Hot 10 brief every Monday and a fully refreshed, re-verified master CSV on the 1st of each month. New states are added as they are verified, at no extra cost.

How does priority scoring work?

Every agency gets a 0–100 priority score from our own scoring model. It weighs the signals that say an agency is ready to buy — how established it is, whether it can take an online booking, how visible it is online — and every row shows the reason for its score in plain English. Scores land in tiers: HOT, WARM, NURTURE, COLD. The recipe itself is ours; what it produces is what you see in the sample.

What counts as a trigger?

Triggers include a newly issued license, a meaningful Google rating change, social activity going quiet, or a visible conversion gap such as no online booking. Each Hot 10 entry includes the change, when it was detected, and a suggested outreach angle.
