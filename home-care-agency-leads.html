Why bought lists fail

You have bought this list before. It did not work then either.

The standard list-buying experience — pay per record, get a spreadsheet, start dialing — breaks in the same three places every time.

01 / STALE

Dead on arrival

Most lists are scraped once and resold for years. Agencies close, rebrand, move, and change administrators — and the spreadsheet never hears about any of it. Your first week of outreach becomes a cleanup project.

02 / NO OWNER

A name, not a person

A business name and a front-desk number is not a lead. Without the owner or administrator — cross-checked against the state license record — your reps pitch whoever answers the phone and hope it travels upward.

03 / NO TIMING

No reason to call today

A flat list treats a fast-growing agency and a dormant one as identical rows. Nothing in it says who has a visible problem a marketer can fix this week — so every call starts cold, in every sense.
