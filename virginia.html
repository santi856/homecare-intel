Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Virginia database (October 2026): home care agencies identified from public records and directories and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Virginia licenses home care organizations through the Department of Health, but VDH publishes no readily searchable public list, so license numbers are recorded on 8 records, only where the agency or a state listing publishes one; license issue dates are not exposed, so the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here yet. Online booking is verified on 50 records and counting. Virginia is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
