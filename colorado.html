Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Colorado database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Colorado licenses home care agencies through CDPHE in two classes (A: skilled, B: non-medical personal care), but the department publishes no usable per-agency roster and license numbers are rarely printed by agencies, so the field holds 4 entries (2 numbers, 2 class-only); license issue dates were published nowhere found, so the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here. Online booking is unchecked on 50 records and Instagram on most Colorado Springs and several franchise locations — a signal-verification pass is the logged first-refresh priority. Colorado is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
