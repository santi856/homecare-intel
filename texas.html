Methodology

Drawn from the OwnerFlight Texas database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. License numbers are recorded on only 4 of the 126 records: Texas does not expose a machine-readable license roster, so license data fills in as verification continues. Booking status is verified on 64 records and counting. Texas is a live OwnerFlight market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
