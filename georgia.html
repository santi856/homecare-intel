Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Georgia database (October 2026): home care agencies identified from public records and directories and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Georgia's Department of Community Health publishes no machine-readable license roster, so this file was assembled from public directories and agency websites; license numbers are recorded on 14 records. Online booking has been classified on 1 record so far — no booking statistic is published for Georgia until verification covers a real share of the file. Mid-size cities outside the five hunted metros (Albany, Athens, Gainesville, Rome, Valdosta) are not yet covered beyond franchise locations. Georgia is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
