Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Arizona database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Arizona scoring is deliberately conservative in this pass: online booking has been classified on 6 records and Instagram presence checked on 59, with the Phoenix-metro signal pass still to come; unverified records were left blank and excluded from every statistic rather than estimated. Arizona does not license non-medical home care agencies, so license numbers appear only where an agency publishes another credential (5 records), and the new-license scoring trigger cannot fire here. Arizona is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
