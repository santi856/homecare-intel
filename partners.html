Partner conduct

Simple rules, enforced.

The whole product is trust in data. Partners protect that — and their commission — by following three rules. The full terms arrive with your partner code, before you promote anything.

Rule 01

Honest claims only

Cite the published brief and the live coverage — Florida today, Texas October 14, a new state every week after. Never promise states, features, or results we have not shipped.

Rule 02

Disclose the commission

A simple “I may earn a commission” line wherever you promote. FTC basics — it protects you, us, and your audience's trust in both.

Rule 03

No spam, ever

No purchased lists, no mass unsolicited outreach, no posting your code where you do not have permission to promote. One spam complaint ends the partnership.
