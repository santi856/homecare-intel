Methodology

This brief is drawn from the HomeCare Intel Florida database (October 2026): licensed home care agencies identified from Florida license records and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated. The full database — owner/administrator names, phones, license numbers, ratings, booking and social signals, and a 0–100 priority score with a plain-English reason per record — is refreshed monthly, with a weekly Hot 10 of the agencies showing the freshest buying triggers.
