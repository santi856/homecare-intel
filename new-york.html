Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel New York database (October 2026): home care agencies identified from public records and directories and cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Google rating data could be found for only 31 of the 114 agencies — New York agencies publish less review data than any other state we track. Booking status is verified on 39 records and counting; 11 records whose source data carried narrative notes in the booking field were excluded from the booking statistic rather than interpreted. New York is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
