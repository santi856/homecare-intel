Methodology

Drawn from the HomeCare Intel Missouri database (October 2026): home care agencies identified and verified by our research team, then cross-checked against each agency's public website, Google Business profile, and social presence. Online-booking and Instagram figures reflect what could be found on each agency's public web presence at verification time; records that could not be verified were left blank rather than estimated, and every statistic on this page is computed only over the records where that signal was actually verified — the denominator is printed next to the figure. Missouri has no statewide licensure for non-medical home care agencies, so the license column is empty by design and the new-license scoring trigger can never fire here; NPI and Medicare numbers were deliberately not recorded as licenses. Southeast Missouri (Cape Girardeau), St. Joseph, and the Bootheel were not hunted in this pass — a gap-fill sweep is logged. Online booking is verified on 50 records. Missouri is a live HomeCare Intel market; the database is refreshed monthly and the weekly Hot 10 tracks fresh buying signals in every live state.
